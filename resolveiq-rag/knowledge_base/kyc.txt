KYC Policy:
- KYC must be completed within 6 months of account opening.
- Re-KYC required every 2 years for high-risk customers.
- Re-KYC required every 8 years for medium-risk customers.
- Re-KYC required every 10 years for low-risk customers.
- Video KYC (V-KYC) accepted as per RBI circular 2020.
- Account freeze due to KYC non-compliance lifted within 7 working days after submission.
- PAN and Aadhaar mandatory for accounts above Rs 50,000 balance.
- KYC rejection complaints resolved within 30 days.
- Aadhaar-based eKYC is instant and paperless.
- Documents accepted: Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License.
