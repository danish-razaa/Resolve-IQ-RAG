UPI Transaction Failure Policy:
- If UPI payment fails and money is debited, the amount must be refunded within 5 business days.
- Customer should report failed UPI transactions within 30 days.
- Transaction ID must be provided for dispute resolution.
- NPCI handles UPI disputes between banks.
- If refund not received in 5 days, customer can raise complaint with bank nodal officer.
- RBI mandates auto-reversal of failed UPI transactions within T+1 day.
- Peak hour failures (7PM-9PM) are common due to server load.
- Customer helpline for UPI: 18001201740

UPI Dispute Resolution Steps:
1. Check transaction status on UPI app
2. If pending more than 1 hour, raise dispute in app
3. Bank has 3 days to respond
4. If unresolved, escalate to NPCI
5. Final escalation to RBI Ombudsman after 30 days
