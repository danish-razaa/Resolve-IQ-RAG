Net Banking Policy:
- Unauthorized net banking transactions: report within 3 working days for zero liability.
- Password reset complaints resolved within 24 hours.
- Account locked after 3 failed login attempts — unlocked via branch or phone banking.
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) mandatory for all transactions above Rs 5000.
- Net banking transfer limits: Rs 10 lakh per day (default).
- IMPS transfer available 24x7, NEFT available 24x7 since 2019.
- RTGS available 7AM-6PM on working days.
- Failed NEFT/RTGS transactions refunded within 2 hours automatically.
