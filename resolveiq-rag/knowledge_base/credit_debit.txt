Credit Card Dispute Policy:
- Unauthorized transactions must be reported within 3 working days for zero liability.
- Chargeback can be raised within 30 days of statement date.
- Bank must resolve chargeback within 45 days.
- Card blocked complaints resolved within 24 hours.
- Credit limit disputes resolved within 7 working days.

Debit Card / ATM Policy:
- ATM cash not dispensed complaints: resolved in 7 working days (own bank ATM).
- Other bank ATM disputes: resolved in 12 working days.
- Compensation of Rs 100 per day after TAT breach.
- Card cloning/skimming complaints: zero liability if reported within 3 days.
- Temporary block can be done via net banking or phone banking instantly.
