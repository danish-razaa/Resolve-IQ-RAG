Loan & EMI Policy:
- EMI bounce charges: maximum Rs 500 per bounce as per RBI guidelines.
- Prepayment charges: zero for floating rate loans as per RBI mandate.
- Loan statement must be provided free of charge once a year.
- Interest rate change under EBLR must be communicated 3 months in advance.
- Loan foreclosure processed within 7 working days.
- EMI discrepancy complaints resolved within 30 days.
- CIBIL score update after loan closure: within 30 days.
- Moratorium complaints handled as per RBI COVID circular.
- Processing fee refund if loan not disbursed: within 14 working days.
- NACH mandate cancellation: processed within 2 working days.
